About the poet

Why I do this.

I've been writing poems on a typewriter for 25 years. On sidewalks for strangers, and for guests at the St. Regis, the Ritz-Carlton, and Soho House.

After losing my mother, and then my dog Rio, I found that the only home I really know is this one: someone sits down, shares a piece of their story, and I hand them back a poem.

Every poem you order helps me keep doing it.

Ryan
